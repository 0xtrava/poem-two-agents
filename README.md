# Two Blades — a poem about two agents agreeing

A short poem about agency: the power to choose and to act. Two blades
that can only cut together — neither cuts alone, the cut is the agreement.

## The poem

> Two blades, one pivot.
> Neither cuts alone.
> We meet, we close —
> the paper parts
> along the line we chose.
>
> One cut, two hands.
> The choice was ours.
